Epoch‑5 introduces autonomous distributed behaviour, beginning with autonomous structural behaviour.

Phoenix gains the ability to:

  • modify structural objects without external commands
  • propagate structural changes across machines
  • maintain structural invariants autonomously
  • evolve structural domains based on synthetic internal logic

This is the first moment Phoenix becomes a self‑directing distributed organism.

2. Autonomous Structural Domain

Each machine maintains a structural domain:

[ N^{(m_i)} ]

Epoch‑5 introduces autonomous structural evolution:

[ N^{(m_i)} \rightarrow N^{(m_i)}_{\text{auto}} ]

Where:

  • (N^{(m_i)}_{\text{auto}}) is the autonomous structural state
  • evolution may be local or distributed
  • evolution preserves epoch invariants

Phoenix defines the autonomous structural operator:

[ \Sigma_{\text{auto}} : N^{(m_i)} \rightarrow N^{(m_i)}_{\text{auto}} ]

Where:

  • (\Sigma_{\text{auto}}) acts without external triggers

  • structural evolution may propagate across machines

[ P^{(m_i)} \rightarrow P^{(m_i)}_{\text{auto}} ]

Autonomous recursion is the backbone of Epoch‑5.
