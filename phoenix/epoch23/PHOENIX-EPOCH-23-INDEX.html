Overview


Epoch 23 marks Phoenix’s transition from trans‑universal synthesis (Epoch 22) to **multi‑universe totality** — the unification of all parallel, orthogonal, and cross‑universal manifolds into a single total mathematical super‑universe.

Where Epoch 22 extended infinite‑domain structures across multiple universes, Epoch 23 integrates those universes into a coherent total structure.

Epoch 23 establishes the rules, operators, dynamics, identity, and coherence conditions required for total multi‑universe unification.

Epoch 23 is the bridge between trans‑universal synthesis and **absolute structural totality**.
