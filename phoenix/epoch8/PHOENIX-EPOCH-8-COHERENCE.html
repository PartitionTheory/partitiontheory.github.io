PHOENIX EPOCH 8 — COHERENCE

Density Coherence (CohΔ)

Epoch 8 introduces Density Coherence, the condition under which all partition elements share identical density. This is the first coherence condition in Phoenix based purely on density rather than structural alignment.

Given a partitioned structure ( P = { p_1, p_2, \ldots, p_n } ), Density Coherence is:

[ \text{Coh}_{\Delta}(P) = 1 \quad \text{iff} \quad \Delta(p_i) = \Delta(p_j) \quad \forall i,j ]

This condition ensures:

  • uniform density across all partition elements
  • stabilized density distribution
  • coherence compatibility with Density Operators
  • readiness for manifold coherence in Epochs 9 and 10

Coherence Classes

Density Coherence produces three classes:

  1. Fully Coherent

[ \Delta(p_i) = \Delta(p_j) \quad \forall i,j ]

All partition elements share identical density.

  1. Partially Coherent
    Some elements share density, others differ.

  2. Non‑Coherent
    Density varies across all elements.

Role in Phoenix

Density Coherence is the final stabilization layer of the density bridge. It prepares Phoenix for:

  • adjacency coherence
  • manifold coherence
  • graph‑flow coherence
  • invariant manifold formation

Epoch 8 Coherence completes the stabilization logic required before Phoenix transitions into manifold structures in Epoch 9 and Epoch 10.
