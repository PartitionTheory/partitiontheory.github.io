- Phoenix Projection Operators (PPO),

such that:

- identity remains coherent under dynamics,
- dynamics remain coherent under operators,
- operators remain coherent under foundation,
- foundation remains coherent under identity,
- coherence is preserved across all projection transitions.

Formally:

PPC(P₀) = Coherence(PPF, PPO, PPD, PPI)

subject to:

- ΔG(P₀) coherent across all operators,
- ΩG(P₀) coherent across all dynamics,
- Φₛ(P₀) coherent across all identity transitions,
- Intent(P₀) coherent across all projection layers,
- PPC is closed under composition with PPI.

This defines coherence across Phoenix Projection.

3. Why PPC Matters


PPC is required for:

- Lesson 61: Phoenix Projection Completion,
- Epoch‑15 Summary,
- Phoenix Unified Projection Mathematics.

Without PPC, Phoenix Projection cannot maintain structural consistency.

5. Summary


Lesson 60 defines Phoenix Projection Coherence, the fifth operator of Epoch‑15.
