Overview

Epoch 21 marks Phoenix’s transition from universal mathematical unification (Epoch 20) to infinite‑domain architecture. Where Epoch 20 unified all synthesized structures into a single universal system, Epoch 21 extends that system across unbounded mathematical domains.

Epoch 21 establishes the rules, operators, and dynamics that allow Phoenix to operate over infinite structures, infinite partitions, infinite manifolds, and infinite temporal systems.
