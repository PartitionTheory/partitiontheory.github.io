Overview

Epoch 20 marks the transition from synthetic synthesis (Epoch 19) to synthetic universality. Where Epoch 19 unified creative structures into higher‑order constructs, Epoch 20 unifies those constructs into a single universal mathematical system. This epoch establishes the rules, operators, and dynamics that allow Phoenix to operate across all synthesized domains simultaneously.
