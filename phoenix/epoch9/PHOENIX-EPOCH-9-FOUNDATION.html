PHOENIX EPOCH 9 — FOUNDATION


Adjacency Foundation (A)



Epoch 9 introduces the Adjacency Foundation, the structural bridge between density
manifolds (Epoch 8) and graph manifolds (Epoch 10). Adjacency defines how partition
elements relate to one another through structural proximity.

Given a partitioned structure \( P = \{ p_1, p_2, \ldots, p_n \} \), adjacency is defined
as a binary relation:



\[
A(p_i, p_j) =
\begin{cases}
1 & \text{if } p_i \text{ is adjacent to } p_j \\
0 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
\]



Adjacency establishes:

- the first Phoenix adjacency relation
- the first adjacency manifold
- the first adjacency operator domain
- the structural precursor to graph edges

Epoch 9 Foundation marks the transition from density‑based structure to adjacency‑based
structure.
