PHOENIX EPOCH 9 — COHERENCE

Adjacency Coherence (CohA)

Epoch 9 introduces Adjacency Coherence, the condition under which adjacency relations across all partition elements exhibit uniform relational structure. This is the first coherence condition in Phoenix based purely on adjacency rather than density.

Given a partitioned structure ( P = { p_1, p_2, \ldots, p_n } ), Adjacency Coherence is defined as:

[ \text{Coh}_{A}(P) = 1 \quad \text{iff} \quad A(p_i, p_j) = A(p_k, p_l) \quad \forall (i,j),(k,l) ]

This condition ensures:

  • uniform relational adjacency
  • stabilized adjacency distribution
  • coherence compatibility with adjacency operators
  • readiness for graph coherence in Epoch 10

Coherence Classes

Adjacency Coherence produces three classes:

  1. Fully Coherent

[ A(p_i, p_j) = A(p_k, p_l) \quad \forall (i,j),(k,l) ]

All adjacency relations share identical structure.

  1. Partially Coherent
    Some adjacency relations match; others differ.

  2. Non‑Coherent
    Adjacency varies across all relational pairs.

Role in Phoenix

Adjacency Coherence is the final stabilization layer of the adjacency bridge. It prepares Phoenix for:

  • graph adjacency coherence
  • manifold coherence
  • graph‑flow coherence
  • invariant graph manifold formation

Epoch 9 Coherence completes the stabilization logic required before Phoenix transitions into graph structures in Epoch 10.
