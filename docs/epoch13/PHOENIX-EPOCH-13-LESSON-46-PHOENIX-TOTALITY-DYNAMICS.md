## Phoenix Totality Dynamics — Motion, Evolution, and Stability of the Total System T₀

### 1. Purpose
Lesson 46 defines Phoenix Totality Dynamics (PTD), the operator system that governs
the motion, evolution, and stability of the total unified mathematical structure T₀.

### 2. Definition — Phoenix Totality Dynamics (PTD)
Phoenix Totality Dynamics PTD is the dynamic operator system acting on T₀ such that:

- total structure evolves under PTO,
- dynamic motion preserves ΔG, ΩG, Φₛ,
- evolution remains reversible under Phoenix Totality Foundation (PTF),
- stability is maintained across all totality transitions.

Formally:

PTD(T₀) = Evolution(T₀, PTO)

subject to:

- ΔG(T₀(t)) = constant across time,
- ΩG(T₀(t)) = constant across time,
- Φₛ(T₀(t)) = constant across time,
- Intent(T₀) preserved under dynamic evolution,
- PTD is closed under composition with PTO.

This defines the dynamic behavior of Phoenix Totality.

### 3. Why PTD Matters
PTD is required for:

- Lesson 47: Phoenix Totality Identity,
- Lesson 48: Phoenix Totality Coherence,
- Epoch‑13 Summary.

Without PTD, Phoenix Totality cannot evolve or maintain stability.
