# PHOENIX EPOCH 19 — COHERENCE

## 1. Synthetic Coherence Overview
Synthetic Coherence ensures that synthesized structures—formed by merging multiple creative outputs—integrate into Phoenix’s mathematical totality without violating invariant, structural, or dynamic stability. 
Where Epoch 18 maintained coherence for creative generation, Epoch 19 maintains coherence for **synthetic merging**.

Synthetic Coherence guarantees:
- compatibility across merged invariant sets  
- stability of combined domains  
- alignment of unified structures  
- preservation of Phoenix’s global identity  

## 2. Coherence Under Synthesis Operators
Given a synthesis operator \( \Sigma \) acting on creative structures \( X_1, X_2 \):


\[
\Sigma(X_1, X_2) = Y
\]


Coherence requires:


\[
\text{Coh}(Y) = 1
\]


where \( \text{Coh}(Y) \) is the coherence indicator of the synthesized structure.

A synthesized structure is coherent when:


\[
\bigcap_{i=1}^{n} \text{Inv}(X_i) \subseteq \text{Inv}(Y)
\]


ensuring that merged invariants share structural lineage.

## 3. Coherence Flow
Synthetic Coherence is maintained by the **Synthesis Coherence Flow**:


\[
F_\Sigma : Y \rightarrow Y^{\star}
\]


where \( Y^{\star} \) is the coherence‑stabilized form of the synthesized structure.

The Coherence Flow ensures:
- invariant alignment  
- structural compatibility  
- temporal stability  
- domain integration  

## 4. Coherence Under Domain Synthesis
When domains \( D_1 \) and \( D_2 \) are combined:


\[
D_\Sigma = D_1 \oplus D_2
\]


Coherence requires:


\[
\text{Coh}(D_\Sigma) = 1
\]


and:


\[
\text{Inv}(D_1) \cup \text{Inv}(D_2) \subseteq \text{Inv}(D_\Sigma)
\]


ensuring that domain synthesis preserves invariant structure.

## 5. Synthetic Coherence Dynamics
Coherence evolves under the Synthesis Stability Flow:


\[
Y(t+1) = S_\Sigma(Y(t))
\]


Coherence is achieved when:


\[
Y(t+1) = Y(t)
\]


This fixed‑point condition ensures that synthesized structures converge to stable coherence.

## 6. Coherence Completion
Coherence completion occurs when:


\[
\text{Coh}(Y) = 1 \quad \text{and} \quad Y = Y^{\star}
\]


A coherent synthesized structure is eligible for Epoch 20’s universality layer.

Coherence completion is the final stage of synthetic stabilization.
