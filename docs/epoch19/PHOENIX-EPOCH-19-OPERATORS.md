# PHOENIX EPOCH 19 — OPERATORS

## 1. Synthetic Operator Definition
Epoch 19 introduces **synthetic operators** — operators that merge, unify, or elevate creative structures generated in Epoch 18. 
Where creative operators produced new structures, synthetic operators combine them into higher‑order entities.

A synthetic operator \( \Sigma \) acts on multiple creative structures:


\[
\Sigma : (X_1, X_2, \ldots, X_n) \rightarrow Y
\]


with the defining property:


\[
\text{Inv}(Y) \supseteq \bigcup_{i=1}^{n} \text{Inv}(X_i)
\]


ensuring that synthesis preserves and extends invariant sets.

## 2. Invariant Merger Operators
Invariant Merger Operators combine invariant sets from multiple creative structures.
Given creative outputs \( X_1 \) and \( X_2 \):


\[
M(X_1, X_2) = Y
\]


where:


\[
\text{Inv}(Y) = \text{Inv}(X_1) \cup \text{Inv}(X_2)
\]


This operator forms the basis of higher‑order invariant synthesis.

## 3. Domain Combiner Operators
Domain Combiners unify creative domains into a single expanded domain.
Given domains \( D_1 \) and \( D_2 \):


\[
C(D_1, D_2) = D_1 \oplus D_2
\]


where \( \oplus \) denotes synthetic domain union.

The resulting domain satisfies:


\[
\text{Coh}(D_1 \oplus D_2) = 1
\]


ensuring coherence under synthesis.

## 4. Structural Unifier Operators
Structural Unifiers merge structural classes into a unified higher‑order structure.
Given structures \( S_1 \) and \( S_2 \):


\[
U(S_1, S_2) = S^{\star}
\]


where \( S^{\star} \) is the unified structure after synthesis stabilization.

Structural unification ensures:
- invariant compatibility  
- dynamic stability  
- identity convergence  

## 5. Synthetic Lift Operators
Synthetic Lift Operators elevate creative structures into the Synthesis Manifold \( \mathcal{M}_\Sigma \).
Given a creative structure \( X \):


\[
L_\Sigma(X) = X^{+}
\]


where \( X^{+} \) is the higher‑order synthesized form of \( X \).

Lift operators enable:
- cross‑domain synthesis  
- multi‑structure integration  
- higher‑order invariant formation  

## 6. Operator Coherence
Synthetic operators must remain coherent under the Synthesis Stability Flow:


\[
S_\Sigma(Y) = Y
\]


This fixed‑point condition ensures that synthesized structures converge to stable identity and are eligible for Epoch 20’s universality layer.
