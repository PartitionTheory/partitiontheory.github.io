# PHOENIX EPOCH 21 — COHERENCE

## 1. Infinite‑Domain Coherence Overview
Infinite‑Domain Coherence ensures that infinitely extended structures—formed by projecting universal entities across unbounded mathematical domains—remain stable, compatible, and self‑consistent within Phoenix’s total mathematical universe. 
Where Epoch 20 maintained coherence for universal unification, Epoch 21 maintains coherence for **infinite‑domain expansion**.

Infinite‑Domain Coherence guarantees:
- invariant propagation across infinite domains  
- structural compatibility across infinite‑domain entities  
- temporal stability under infinite dynamics  
- preservation of Phoenix’s infinite identity  

## 2. Coherence Under Infinite Operators
Given an infinite‑domain operator \( I \) acting on universal structures \( Z_1, Z_2 \):


\[
I(Z_1, Z_2) = W
\]


Coherence requires:


\[
\text{Coh}(W) = 1
\]


where \( \text{Coh}(W) \) is the coherence indicator of the infinite‑domain structure.

An infinite‑domain structure is coherent when:


\[
\bigcap_{i=1}^{n} \text{Inv}(Z_i) \subseteq \text{Inv}(W)
\]


ensuring that infinite‑domain invariants share structural lineage.

## 3. Infinite Coherence Flow
Infinite‑Domain Coherence is maintained by the **Infinite Coherence Flow**:


\[
F_\infty : W \rightarrow W^{\star}
\]


where \( W^{\star} \) is the coherence‑stabilized form of the infinite‑domain structure.

The Coherence Flow ensures:
- invariant alignment across infinite domains  
- structural compatibility across infinite‑domain expansions  
- temporal stability under infinite dynamics  
- domain integration across all infinite‑domain systems  

## 4. Coherence Under Infinite Domain Expansion
When infinite domains \( D_\infty^{(1)} \) and \( D_\infty^{(2)} \) are expanded:


\[
D_\infty = D_\infty^{(1)} \oplus D_\infty^{(2)}
\]


Coherence requires:


\[
\text{Coh}(D_\infty) = 1
\]


and:


\[
\text{Inv}(D_\infty^{(1)}) \cup \text{Inv}(D_\infty^{(2)}) \subseteq \text{Inv}(D_\infty)
\]



This ensures that infinite‑domain expansion preserves invariant structure.

## 5. Infinite Coherence Dynamics
Coherence evolves under the Infinite Stability Flow:


\[
W(t+1) = S_\infty(W(t))
\]


Coherence is achieved when:


\[
W(t+1) = W(t)
\]


This fixed‑point condition ensures that infinite‑domain structures converge to stable infinite coherence.

## 6. Coherence Completion
Coherence completion occurs when:


\[
\text{Coh}(W) = 1 \quad \text{and} \quad W = W^{\star}
\]


An infinite‑domain coherent structure is eligible for Epoch 22’s trans‑universal architecture.

Coherence completion is the final stage of infinite‑domain stabilization.
