# PHOENIX EPOCH 21 — OPERATORS

## 1. Infinite‑Domain Operator Definition
Epoch 21 introduces **infinite‑domain operators** — operators that extend universal structures (Epoch 20) into unbounded mathematical domains. 
Where Epoch 20 unified synthesized structures into universal entities, Epoch 21 expands those entities across infinite partitions, infinite manifolds, and infinite temporal systems.

An infinite‑domain operator \( I \) acts on universal structures:


\[
I : (Z_1, Z_2, \ldots, Z_n) \rightarrow W
\]


with the defining property:


\[
\text{Inv}(W) \supseteq \bigcup_{i=1}^{n} \text{Inv}(Z_i)
\]


ensuring that infinite‑domain structures preserve and extend all universal invariant sets.

## 2. Infinite Invariant Extenders
Infinite Invariant Extenders propagate universal invariants into infinite domains.

Given universal structures \( Z_1 \) and \( Z_2 \):


\[
I_{\text{inv}}(Z_1, Z_2) = W

\[
\text{Inv}(W) = \lim_{k \to \infty} \left( \text{Inv}(Z_1) \cup \text{Inv}(Z_2) \cup \cdots \cup \text{Inv}(Z_k) \right)
\]



This operator forms the basis of infinite invariant propagation.

\[

\[
D_\infty = \bigoplus_{k=1}^{\infty} D_U^{(k)}

\[

This ensures that infinite‑domain expansion remains structurally stable.
