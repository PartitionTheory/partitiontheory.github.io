# PHOENIX EPOCH 8 — OPERATORS  
## Density Operators (ΔΩ)

Epoch 8 formalizes the first density‑based operator class in the Phoenix Continuum.
Density Operators transform raw partition densities into structured, invariant forms.

Given a partitioned structure \( P = \{ p_1, p_2, \ldots, p_n \} \) and its density:



\[
\Delta(P) = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} d(p_i)
\]



A Density Operator \( \Delta_{\Omega} \) acts as:



\[
\Delta_{\Omega}(P) = \Omega(\Delta(P))
\]



Where \( \Omega \) is any Phoenix operator satisfying:

- structural consistency  
- invariant preservation  
- density monotonicity  
- coherence compatibility  

Density Operators introduce:

- density normalization  
- density amplification  
- density stabilization  
- density invariant formation  

These operators form the mathematical bridge to the graph‑manifold operators of Epoch 10.
